# Business Insights

## Insight 1 (Q16 – Revenue by Product Category)

### Finding

Mobile Phones generated the highest revenue at **KSh 6,942,060.65**, followed by Computing at **KSh 6,596,221.70** and Electronics at **KSh 5,515,359.20**. Accessories generated the lowest revenue at **KSh 1,077,496.10**.

### Why It Matters

Mobile Phones are the company's primary revenue driver and represent the strongest source of sales. The strong performance of Mobile Phones and Computing indicates high customer demand for technology products.

### Recommendation

Prioritize inventory availability and marketing campaigns for Mobile Phones and Computing products. Bundle accessories such as chargers, keyboards, and earbuds with major purchases to increase basket size and overall revenue.

## Insight 2 (Q25 – Revenue by Customer Location)

### Finding

Nairobi contains many of the company's highest-spending customers. Customer 33 generated **KSh 1,145,917.05**, Customer 39 generated **KSh 919,002.40**, and Customer 19 generated **KSh 896,674.90** in revenue.

### Why It Matters

The concentration of high-value customers in Nairobi suggests that it is the company's most important market. A large portion of revenue is generated from customers in this region.

### Recommendation

Strengthen customer loyalty initiatives and targeted marketing campaigns in Nairobi while expanding successful sales strategies into other growing markets such as Mombasa, Nakuru, Kisumu, and Eldoret.

## Insight 3 (Q33 – Profitability by Product Category)

### Finding

Mobile Phones generated the highest profit at **KSh 728,760.65**, followed by Computing at **KSh 700,021.70**. However, Accessories achieved the highest profit margin at **42.39%**, generating **KSh 456,746.10** in profit from only **KSh 1,077,496.10** in revenue.

### Why It Matters

Revenue alone does not determine business success. While Mobile Phones and Computing produce higher sales volumes, Accessories generate substantially more profit for every shilling earned.

### Recommendation

Increase cross-selling and upselling of accessories by offering bundles and checkout recommendations. Encouraging customers to purchase accessories alongside phones and computers can significantly boost profitability.

## Insight 4 (Q40 – Revenue Growth and Seasonality)

### Finding

Revenue experienced significant fluctuations throughout the analysis period. The strongest growth occurred in **June 2026**, when revenue increased by **72.97%** to **KSh 2,038,372.05**. Revenue peaked in **August 2026** at **KSh 2,242,033.90**. In contrast, **January 2026** recorded an **84.09% decline**, with revenue falling to **KSh 322,432.95**.

### Why It Matters

Large swings in revenue create challenges for forecasting, budgeting, and inventory planning. The strong performance during mid-2026 suggests seasonal demand patterns or successful promotional activities.

### Recommendation

Investigate the factors that contributed to strong performance in June and August 2026 and replicate those strategies during weaker months. Implement targeted promotions before traditionally slow periods to stabilize revenue.

## Insight 5 (Q45 – Customer Value Segmentation)

### Finding

A relatively small group of customers generates a substantial share of total revenue. The highest-value customer, **Brian Chebet (Customer 33)**, spent **KSh 848,118.75**. Several other customers, including Customers 93, 19, 31, and 65, each spent more than **KSh 698,000** and were classified as High Value customers.

### Why It Matters

The business relies heavily on a core group of high-value customers. Losing even a few of these customers could significantly reduce revenue. Medium- and Low-Value customers also represent opportunities for future growth.

### Recommendation

Develop loyalty and retention programs for High-Value customers, including personalized offers, exclusive promotions, and priority customer support. Create targeted campaigns to encourage Medium- and Low-Value customers to increase their spending and move into higher-value segments.
